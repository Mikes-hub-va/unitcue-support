UnitCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

UnitCue is designed to work locally on your device.

Data we collect

UnitCue does not collect personal data, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or account information in version 1.0.

Camera and shelf-tag scanning

When you use the scanner, UnitCue uses Apple-provided device frameworks to recognize text from shelf tags. UnitCue does not upload camera images to our servers.

Price history

Products, stores, prices, and dates that you save are stored locally on your device.

Third parties

Version 1.0 does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the UnitCue support page.
